CFO Services

Moving beyond basic accounting to investor-ready reporting — strategic financial leadership for growing businesses without the cost of a full-time CFO.

Your Outsourced Chief Financial Officer

We act as your outsourced Chief Financial Officer — helping SMEs transition from founder-led finance to institutional-grade systems. Whether you're scaling operations, preparing for your first round of funding, or establishing the financial discipline that attracts debt and equity partners, our CFO services bridge the gap between where your business is today and where it needs to be.

Our CFO engagements run on BooksIQ — our in-house finance intelligence platform that turns your Tally and GST data into real-time dashboards, compliance alerts and decision-ready MIS.

Core Focus Areas

Financial Strategy & Planning

Build budgets, forecasts, and cash flow discipline. Financial planning and treasury management to guide business growth.

Performance Management & MIS

Deliver monthly dashboards and decision-ready MIS, powered by our BooksIQ platform. Real-time performance monitoring and strategic reporting.

Fundraising & Investor Readiness

Prepare investment pitch decks and financial models. Support investor discussions and comprehensive due diligence processes.

Governance, Compliance & Controls

Structure controls, manage liquidity, and mitigate financial risks. Build robust governance framework and regulatory compliance.

The Missing CFO Link

When Does Your SME Need a CFO?

Most SME owners don't realise they need a CFO until they face one of these situations.

Investors are asking for financials you can't produce
Revenue is growing but cash flow is unpredictable
You're preparing for debt or equity fundraising
Board or stakeholders need professional MIS reporting
You need to scale from Excel sheets to real financial systems
Compliance and statutory deadlines are being missed
You're evaluating an M&A transaction or strategic partnership
