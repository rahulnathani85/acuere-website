About Acuere Consultancy

A Mumbai-based management consulting firm built on technical expertise and professional integrity.

Our Firm

Acuere Consultancy is a management consulting firm based in Mumbai, India. We are committed to delivering independent, regulatory-compliant solutions backed by deep technical expertise and unwavering professional integrity.

Our Approach

We bring together rigorous analytical capabilities with a deep understanding of the Indian regulatory landscape — spanning the Companies Act, Income Tax Act, FEMA regulations, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Every engagement is treated with the same thoroughness and stakeholder-focused communication, whether it's a single valuation or a multi-phase advisory mandate.

Our Heritage

With professional training at Big 4 consulting firms and over 14 years of hands-on experience, our team operates at the intersection of institutional-quality standards and practical, client-first execution. We are deeply rooted in the Indian business context while adhering to global best practices.
