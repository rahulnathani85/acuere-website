## STEP-BY-STEP: Google Search Console Setup

### Step 1: Go to Google Search Console
1. Visit https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with your Google account (use your business Gmail if you have one)

### Step 2: Add Your Property
1. Click "Add Property"
2. Choose "URL prefix" method
3. Enter: `https://acuereconsultancy.com`
4. Click "Continue"

### Step 3: Verify Ownership (DNS Method - Recommended)
1. Google will give you a TXT record to add
2. Go to GoDaddy DNS Management for acuereconsultancy.com
3. Add a new TXT record:
   - **Type**: TXT
   - **Name**: @ (or leave blank)
   - **Value**: The verification string Google provides (starts with "google-site-verification=...")
   - **TTL**: 1 Hour
4. Go back to Google Search Console and click "Verify"
5. It may take a few minutes to propagate

### Step 4: Submit Your Sitemap
1. In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"
4. Google will start crawling your pages

### Step 5: Request Indexing
1. Go to "URL Inspection" in the left menu
2. Enter your homepage URL: `https://acuereconsultancy.com/`
3. Click "Request Indexing"
4. Repeat for each important page:
   - `https://acuereconsultancy.com/valuations.html`
   - `https://acuereconsultancy.com/cfo-services.html`
   - `https://acuereconsultancy.com/about.html`
   - `https://acuereconsultancy.com/contact.html`
