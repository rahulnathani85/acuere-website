Deal Advisory

Modelling & Transaction Support

Acuere provides focused financial modelling and transaction advisory for M&A, acquisitions, and capital raising initiatives. We develop rigorous financial models that underpin deal evaluation, support valuation discussions, and facilitate informed decision-making for boards and investors.

Our expertise spans model development, scenario analysis, and documentation that meets investment committee and regulatory standards.

Key Services

Financial Projection Models

Develop multi-year financial projections incorporating growth scenarios, cost assumptions, and capital requirements. Models built for board-level review and investor presentations.

Deal Evaluation Support

Quantify deal economics, synergy potential, and return on investment. Provide independent analysis to support acquisition target evaluation and negotiation strategy.

Preparation of Investment Memos

Develop investment memos that combine market context, financial analysis, and risk assessment for internal stakeholder review and decision-making.

Underwriting Support

Support fundraising rounds with financial modelling, investor deck preparation, and valuation analysis to strengthen capital raising outcomes.
